Hit rate is the share of recent ticks that would have won the contract. A hot streak does not change the odds of the next tick.

How the signals work

For each market the app keeps the last digit of every price tick. A signal appears when a contract’s hit rate over the last 20 ticks (50 for Digit Match) sits well above its base rate. The bot then waits for your trigger digits and places the runs you set. A stronger signal suggests more runs, from 4 to 12, and Trade now fills that number in for you.

Each tick’s last digit is random. A hot streak does not make the next tick more likely to win, and payouts leave Deriv a margin, so a bot can lose money even when signals look strong. Practise first and set a stop loss.
