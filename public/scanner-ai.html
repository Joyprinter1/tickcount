Scanner AI

Press Scan. It reads the last 1,000 ticks of every Volatility market, ranks the Even/Odd and Over/Under setups, and loads the best one into the bot. It counts digits. It cannot know the next digit.

Preview with sample ticks, not live markets. Open Scanner AI inside Joytrader for live data.

Look for

Even/Odd is ready when 4 of the 5 Even (or Odd) digits are each at 10% or more. Over/Under is ready when the two most common digits are both on the winning side and the top digit is at 12% or more.
